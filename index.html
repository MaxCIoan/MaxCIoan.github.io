Maximilian Cozzone Portfolio

Practical work, written clearly

Technology hooked me early. At seven, online games and PC games made me curious about what was happening behind the screen; building my first gaming PC made that curiosity physical, where every upgrade proved how much speed, power, and precision matter.

Favorite Tech Film

Who Am I: No System Is Safe

That curiosity moved into Raspberry Pi experiments, Linux installs, CTF practice, and hands-on security labs where I could break, rebuild, document, and understand systems instead of only using them.

I like the message because "no system is safe" is not just a movie line. However well defended a target is, there is always a path to test, harden, or expose it, which is why discipline, curiosity, and constant improvement matter.
